# ADR-0013: Design language: Star Atlas

- **Status:** Accepted; motion and space-effect rules amended by [ADR-0016](ADR-0016-cinematic-motion.md)
- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Deciders:** Robert Gerald Senasin

## Context

Gerald wants a space/cosmos game aesthetic that isn't overpowering or bland, and the explanation needs a natural visual form.

## Options considered

1. Plain dashboard
2. Full sci-fi HUD
3. Star atlas: constellations as reasons, quiet panels

## Decision

"Star Atlas" (11_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md): Deep Field (dark) and Atlas Paper (light) themes; constellation lines = attributions; one accent per view; space effects ≤ 10% of visual weight; reduced motion respected.

## Consequences

A memorable identity where the visual metaphor is the explanation itself; accessibility targets are explicit.
